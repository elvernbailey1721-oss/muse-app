# NEONFANG

**15-Minute Short Screenplay — Draft v1 (2026-10-01)**
Adapted from the locked manuscript `neonfang-draft-v3.txt`.
Sparse-dialogue action-horror. Theme: *Patience beats firepower.*

---

FADE IN:

## SEQUENCE 1 — LANDFALL (0:00–1:30)

**EXT. EVERGLADES CHANNEL — NIGHT**

A hurricane eats the world. KAI (30s, athletic, cheek scar, black tactical gear, ponytail plastered to her skull) poles a skiff through black water. Rain hammers her poncho.

She finds a POACHER'S SKIFF wedged in cypress roots — abandoned. Inside the hull: deep parallel TALON SCARS, clawed from within. No blood. No bodies.

She touches the scars. Then looks up —

Through a break in the mangroves: the MIAMI SKYLINE, every neon sign still burning against the storm. Pink. Blue. Gold.

Kai's eyes narrow. The lights aren't just on.

They're *calling*.

**TITLE CARD: NEONFANG** — then it burns away like a sign shorting out.

## SEQUENCE 2 — THE PACT (1:30–3:30)

**EXT. FLOODED MARINA — NIGHT**

Knee-deep black water. STALKERS — loping mutated iguanas — swarm the base of a buzzing pink **TROPICANA** sign, claws scraping glass. They ignore everything but the light.

Kai, behind an overturned dumpster, watches. She hefts a length of REBAR — hurls it onto a sputtering transformer casing. CLANG.

The Stalkers snap toward the sound, converge — the big one touches the rebar and completes the circuit. BLUE-WHITE ERUPTION. The pack convulses, chain-lightning through the water. Then dark. Steam. Husks sinking.

Kai hasn't fired a shot.

Heavy sloshing. DUKE (50s, bald, gray-flecked beard, burn scar at his temple, olive shirt, bandolier, pump shotgun) emerges from the gloom. Surveys the carnage.

> **DUKE**
> You the one responsible for this?

> **KAI**
> They were a problem. Now they're fertilizer.

A chittering SHRIEK — RUNNERS pour in, drawn by the kill. Duke pivots, FIRES — pump, fires again. Muzzle flashes strobe. The horde surges *over* its dead, faster, angrier. The fire is a magnet.

Kai doesn't shoot. She moves — parallel to the fight — to a half-drowned GENERATOR. Kills it. The sudden quiet reveals a new sound: the fuel pumps' emergency override, WHINING, intake aimed at the Runners. They falter, turn toward it.

Kai scrambles onto the fuel-station roof, aims Old Betsy — not at the creatures — at the rusted clamp on the Tropicana's power conduit. FIRES. The severed cable whips free, plunges into the water around the intake.

The water lights up from within. The pump mists gasoline across the electrified surface —

A silent, absolute FIREBALL sucks the air out of the marina. The Runners convert from flesh to vapor without a sound.

Duke stands frozen, shotgun limp.

> **DUKE**
> My way... I would have died there.

> **KAI**
> Patience beats firepower.

Duke produces a small hardened device. A red diode pulses: *thump... thump... thump.*

> **DUKE**
> The Neon Basilisk. Twelve million, dead, confirmed. It locked onto this frequency at the lab where my team died. We shot first. That's how you lose.

> **KAI**
> It's not just drawn to the light. It's *eating* it.

> **DUKE**
> I don't have a plan anymore. Just a ghost and a price tag.

Kai looks at the diode. Then at the skyline — every sign a lure.

> **KAI**
> One night. We find where it feeds. And we don't fight it. We turn the whole damn city against it.

## SEQUENCE 3 — THE GRID DIES (3:30–5:30)

**EXT. FLOODED BOULEVARD / FLAMINGO GRAND — NIGHT**

A wave of DARKNESS rolls across the drowned city — blocks winking out in sequence. Not random. *Drinking.*

One sign keeps burning: the Flamingo Grand's giant pink-and-blue FLAMINGO. At its base, around the substation, light pours *downward* into something coiled and bus-sized.

The beacon on Duke's vest STUTTERS — thump, thump-*thump*, thump — copying itself. The Basilisk is throwing his signal back. Baiting him.

Duke's shotgun comes up on pure reflex — BLAM. The muzzle flash BENDS, visibly, pouring down into the coiled shape like water down a drain. The shape gets *brighter*.

> **KAI**
> Stop feeding it!

She drags him behind a drowned newsstand as the Basilisk uncoils — thirty feet of black water and white current — and turns toward the flash like a flower to the sun.

> **KAI** (cont'd)
> We go in from the dark. From underneath.

## SEQUENCE 4 — THE THROAT (5:30–7:00)

**INT. FLAMINGO GRAND SERVICE CORRIDOR — NIGHT**

Absolute dark. Kai and Duke move by touch, shotguns slung — light is a dinner bell.

Duke's handset crackles.

> **NELL (V.O., radio; dry as chalk)**
> Testing. You two alive down there? I'm across the boulevard, high ground. Also I'm in the building's management system, because their password was "flamingo123." Try not to die; the paperwork would be a nightmare.

> **NELL (V.O.)** (cont'd)
> Your friend is in the ducts. It's moving toward you.

Skittering overhead. Then — a TELEPHONE RINGING from a dead wall phone, cord long cut. Two short bursts. No power. No system.

> **KAI** (breath)
> It's bait. Don't.

The ringing stops. Kai raises a flat palm: *hold.* She counts in the dark —

One... two... three...

A filament of blue-white current unspools from a duct vent, tasting the air — passes six inches over Duke's bowed head —

...seven... eight... nine...

It withdraws. Two fingers: *move.* Three paces — and the ceiling where Duke stood EXPLODES downward, a barbed current-wreathed limb grasping empty air.

It learned the ten-count.

> **KAI**
> Keep moving! Don't look at it! Count and move!

They run the throat in bursts — freeze, three paces, freeze — while dead fixtures flare and every dead phone in the corridor rings like a carillon.

A steel door: **SIGN ACCESS — AUTHORIZED ONLY.**

> **KAI**
> The sign is the mouth. We just walked into the throat.

## SEQUENCE 5 — THE STAIR (7:00–8:30)

**INT. STAIRWELL — NIGHT**

Twenty stories of wet concrete. Duke climbs, shotgun slung, hands empty.

> **NELL (V.O.)**
> It's chilled its skin to ambient — thermal's blind. But every twelve seconds it has to vent excess current or cook itself. Half a second where the camouflage drops.

> **DUKE**
> Twelve seconds. Half a second.

> **NELL (V.O.)**
> One more thing. The sign's main feed runs through a utility chase behind the stairwell wall on seventeen. Get Kai to that chase — she can wire the whole trap from there.

Kai stops at a sprinkler valve. Cracks it. The landing fills with fine MIST —

— and the stairwell reveals its passenger: FIFTEEN FEET of Basilisk coiled around the column two flights up, drawn in a million lit droplets. Black glass and white fire. Head like a car hood. Waiting.

> **KAI**
> Nell — the sign's feed, the east riser. If we ground the substations and kick the sign to full, where does the vent go?

> **NELL (V.O.)**
> The rooftop broadcast towers. It'd throw its own lightning at the sky.

> **KAI**
> A cage with no walls.

Above them the Basilisk VENTS — a silent blinding pulse, camouflage dropping for one half-second — then invisible again, climbing toward the roof.

> **KAI** (cont'd)
> Let it go. Let it go right where we want it.

## SEQUENCE 6 — THE MIRRORS (8:30–10:30)

**INT. BALLROOM — NIGHT**

A hall of mirrors. Floor-to-ceiling glass, smoked-mirror ceiling. The rooftop sign's pink-blue light pours through the skylights, multiplying into a hundred flamingos, a hundred Kais.

The Basilisk hunts through the reflections — a ripple moving against the light, in three mirrors at once and none.

Glass EXPLODES left — it struck Kai's reflection. The real Kai is already moving.

> **KAI**
> Don't fire at the mirrors!

Duke spins — muzzle flash blooming — and puts a blast into a wall of glass. The mirrors drink it. The ripple swells, brighter, faster.

> **DUKE**
> It's everywhere!

> **KAI**
> It's nowhere! Listen for the cycle!

She closes her eyes. Counts. The hum peaks, strains —

...ten. Eleven. Twelve —

> **KAI** (cont'd)
> NOW!

Eyes open — the camouflage drops: the real Basilisk coiled around the chandelier mount, venting raw current into the skylight.

Kai doesn't fire Old Betsy. She fires the room — her hand finds the detonator she'd wired on seventeen and slams it. The grounded substations dump; current leaps *past* the Basilisk, up toward the rooftop towers — and the thing, mid-vent, catches its own discharge on the surge.

Its scream is a transformer dying — irregular tearing crackle, glass and lightning. Mirrors blow out in sequence. Black current sheets off its scales as it tears free and flees up through the skylight housing.

Silence. A cave of broken glass.

> **DUKE**
> You didn't shoot it.

> **KAI**
> Twelve seconds did it for me.

Above them, the Flamingo sign flickers — pink, blue — and dies.

> **KAI** (cont'd)
> It's hurt, it's hungry, and it's going up. We finish the cage before it learns a new trick.

## SEQUENCE 7 — THE DECK (10:30–11:30)

**EXT. POOL ROOF — NIGHT**

Open sky, black water. The rooftop pool stretches forty feet under the clouds. At the far end, a blue neon MARLIN sign buzzes — the last light on the roof.

Six SPITTERS on the skybridge, throat sacs pulsing. A pack of Runners flowing up the east stairs. All converging on the neon like moths.

Duke behind overturned lounge chairs, shotgun loaded, twelve rounds on the bandolier. Every instinct screams *fire.*

Behind him, Kai works the marlin sign's transformer, wiring it to the flooded filtration trench — unhurried.

> **KAI**
> They'll come across the deck. They can't help it.

> **DUKE**
> And then?

> **KAI**
> Bait. Wait. Drown.

The first Runner hits the deck — Duke's finger finds the trigger — and *stops.* He counts.

One... two... three...

A Spitter hauls over the rail. The pack flows around the pool's edge, eyes on the marlin. Duke counts, hands shaking. The old Duke fired at four.

...eight... nine... ten...

The deck is black with them. The filtration trench gurgles.

> **KAI** (whisper)
> Now.

Duke doesn't fire his shotgun. He fires the *sign* — kicks the breaker Kai wired. The marlin's whole blue charge dumps into the flooded trench at once.

The deck lights up like a struck lake. Six Spitters, eleven Runners — dropped in a single silent blue flash. Not shot. Stopped.

The marlin dies with them.

Duke stands in the dark, shotgun unfired, and laughs — short, disbelieving.

> **DUKE**
> I didn't shoot.

> **KAI**
> You waited. Ten counts. Look at you, learning.

A final Runner drags itself toward the stairwell. Duke raises the shotgun on reflex —

Kai's hand comes down on the barrel, gently, pushing it to the floor.

> **KAI** (cont'd)
> Let it go. Let it carry the story downstairs. Fear's a weapon too.

From the stairwell below rises the Basilisk's pulse — *thump. Thump-thump. Thump.*

> **KAI** (cont'd)
> Garage. Service level. It'll run for the dark and the current. We meet it there.

Duke racks his shotgun — then, deliberately, thumbs the shells out one by one into his pocket. Keeps two. Just in case.

> **DUKE**
> Patience.

## SEQUENCE 8 — THE MOUTH (11:30–12:30)

**INT. PARKING GARAGE, SERVICE LEVEL — NIGHT**

Total dark. Sealed-box black. The only light in the world: the red diode on Duke's beacon, held out like a lantern.

*Thump... thump... thump.* — and the dark answers — *thump. Thump-thump. Thump.*

> **DUKE** (whisper)
> It's mocking me.

> **KAI**
> It's hunting you.

A pressure wave. The air thickens. Fifteen feet of black glass and white fire uncoils from the ceiling pipes — camouflage dropped, too hurt and hungry to hide. Node-clusters of light where eyes should be, fixed on the red pulse.

It strikes —

Three seconds in frames: the head lancing at the light — Duke sidestepping, patience in motion — his arm driving the beacon housing straight into the gaping node-cluster of its mouth — the current-ringed jaws clamping on the housing — Duke tearing free as the diode rips loose and LODGES, glowing, in the translucent flesh of the Basilisk's mouth.

The monster rears, thrashing, the diode strobing from inside its own skull — *thump, thump, thump* — its stolen rhythm trapped in its head, broadcasting its position to the world.

Duke hits the floor, glove torn, knuckles bloody, hand intact.

> **DUKE** (gasping)
> Tell me that worked.

> **KAI**
> Oh, Duke. It worked beautifully.

The Basilisk flees — not up, but *down*, crashing through a maintenance door, seeking the deep current, the one place the signal can't follow.

Except it's inside.

> **KAI** (cont'd)
> Every thump is a map. We follow the money — all the way down to the junction.

## SEQUENCE 9 — THE JUNCTION (12:30–14:00)

**INT. SUB-BASEMENT 2, TRANSFORMER VAULT — NIGHT**

A cage of steel bars around the Flamingo sign's main transformer — a humming hulk of malevolent current. The air is ozone; hair stands on end. This is the Basilisk's larder.

*Thump... thump... thump* — through the walls now. From inside the monster's head.

> **KAI**
> Overload. Not a cut — a feedback loop. We trip primary and secondary, leave tertiary online, ground the transformers to the water main. This whole tunnel becomes a toaster oven.

Kai shatters a fire-axe case with Old Betsy's butt. First swing — the primary breaker's housing splits, sparks erupting. Second swing — the copper core exposed, writhing like a severed nerve.

Together they wrestle a grounding strap onto the posts, bare hands, concrete glowing faint red around the bolts.

The water at the transformer's base begins to churn — a vortex — and the Basilisk RISES: not a body but a serpentine column of black water and pure current, its head a node of blinding white light. It ignores the cable, ignores the posts. It turns toward the crushed housing in Duke's fist — toward the *shape* of the signal burning inside its own skull.

> **DUKE**
> Now, Kai!

Kai swings the axe — not at the creature — at the WATER MAIN. The pipe bursts. A high-pressure jet slams into the Basilisk's shimmering form.

The feedback loop completes. The severed cable's power, the grounded transformers, the ruptured main — the room becomes a crucible. Silent, blinding DETONATION. Duke is thrown — white glare, a skull-splitting whine — then dark.

Silence. Ringing. Cold water.

The transformer is a blackened skeleton. The water is still.

And in the thing's ruined mouth, the diode still pulses — *thump... thump... thump* — a twelve-million-dollar heartbeat in a dead god's skull.

Proof of the kill.

And a warning: the ping keeps broadcasting — and something out in the dark water has heard it.

Kai breathes. Shallow. Stubborn. Alive.

## SEQUENCE 10 — GOLDEN EYES (14:00–15:00)

**EXT. BISCAYNE BAY — DAWN**

Gray, exhausted dawn. A work skiff cuts a white wake through tea-colored water. NELL (50s, wiry, headset, cigarette going in the rain) at the tiller. Kai and Duke sit wrecked in the stern. Behind them, Miami: black, silent, every neon sign dead for the first time in Kai's memory.

> **NELL**
> Whatever you did down there, he came back different.

The skiff rounds the point. The bay opens — flat, gray, endless. The wake smooths behind them... and smooths further, until the surface is glass.

The drizzle still falls. The water doesn't ripple.

> **KAI** (quiet)
> Nell. Kill the engine.

Silence rolls over the bay like a held breath. No wind. No gulls.

Then Kai feels it — not hears — a deep slow pressure rolling up through the hull, through her boots, through her bones. A seismic thump. Again. Again. A heartbeat. Slow. Patient. From below.

Under the glass-still water, something rises — the surface never breaks — a vast dark mass ascending through the gray deep.

Two GOLDEN EYES open underwater. Impossibly wide apart. Each the size of a skiff. They fix on the little boat.

This thing was here before the city. Before the grid. Before the neon. The Basilisk was a symptom — and this is the patient.

Duke wakes up.

Kai stands, slowly. Lifts Old Betsy. Thumbs a shell into the magazine — *click-clack* — and raises the shotgun to her shoulder, sighting at the space between the golden eyes.

And she waits.

SMASH CUT TO BLACK.

**NEONFANG**

FADE OUT.

## PRODUCTION NOTES (not for shooting)

**Locations (all achievable as dressed sets / Kling plates):** Everglades channel, flooded marina, flooded boulevard, hotel service corridor, stairwell, mirrored ballroom, pool roof, parking garage, transformer vault, skiff on open water. Ten setups — tight but shootable.

**Cast:** Kai, Duke, Nell (voice + final scene). No extras required.

**Creature FX:** Basilisk (current-serpent, camouflage/invisible states — Kling-friendly: mostly light, water, and suggestion); Stalkers/Runners/Spitters (packs, largely silhouettes); Gator God (two golden eyes + water displacement only — never fully shown).

**Sound motifs (canon):** neon = irregular crackle, never a tonal siren; the Basilisk's scream = dying transformer; the beacon diode = the film's heartbeat (thump... thump... thump). Phone-band-forward mixing throughout.

**Signage:** English/Latin only. No kanji, no Japanese text.

**Theme, stated once, shown throughout:** *Patience beats firepower.*
