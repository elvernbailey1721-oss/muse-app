# NEONFANG — Claude Context Pack

**What this is:** Everything about the NEONFANG movie, packaged for Claude AI.

**The movie:** NEONFANG is a 15-minute action-horror short (with feature ambitions).
Theme: *Patience beats firepower.* Style: 90s OVA cel-shaded anime.
Goal: a little-to-no-money film made to break records (two billion viewers).

**Characters (canon — keep identical in every shot/scene):**
- **KAI** (30s, athletic, cheek scar, black tactical gear, ponytail, shotgun) — the lead.
- **DUKE** — her partner. Learning patience over firepower.
- **The BASILISK** — the kaiju. A beacon diode is lodged in its mouth, still pinging.
- **NELL** — radio operator, key supporting role.

**Key canon facts:**
- $12 million contract drives the plot.
- The junction kill is the climax.
- Final hook: golden eyes / the Gator God.
- 10 sequences: Landfall, The Pact, The Grid Dies, The Throat, The Stair,
  The Mirrors, The Deck, The Mouth, The Junction, Golden Eyes.
